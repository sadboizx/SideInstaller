Note

SideInstaller is a free iOS sideloading platform. Sideload third-party apps and tweaks not available on the App Store — with one-tap signing, delivered over the air. The included anti-revoke DNS profile keeps revoked certificates operational. No computer or jailbreak required.

1

Setup

Install DNS Profile
  1. Tap Install DNS Profile above and tap Allow when Safari asks to download the configuration profile.
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded (or go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management), then tap Install.
  3. Apply the DNS: in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, open DNS and make sure SideInstaller is selected. Keep it enabled until setup is complete.
2

Signing

Pick an app and tap Sign, then choose a certificate — signing is automatic.

How to install
  1. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad in Safari.
  2. Pick an app above and tap Sign.
  3. Wait while the app signs automated — once it's ready, tap Install on the prompt.
  4. The app now installs on your Home Screen — wait until the app is ready.
  5. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the developer profile, and Trust it.
  6. Launch the app. If one certificate is revoked or won't open, just sign app with another certificate.
3

Completion

You're all set.

That's it. Your signed apps appear on the Home Screen - go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → ENTERPRISE APP → Trust

IMPORTANT: Once an app is installed, leave the DNS Profile exactly as it is — DNS must stay ON at all times. It is the only thing standing between these revoked certificates and Apple's servers: Disable OR Delete it your apps stop working. Likewise, Do not use a VPN — as VPN traffic bypasses the DNS filter and your apps may get revoked.
